## Decision

uncovered hunks 23→0, lint errors 0→0 in every case — but the blind A/B
prefers v2 in only 2 of 6 cases (baseline 2, split 2), below the 4 needed to
ship. Moved: rationale_depth 3.00→3.50, concreteness 2.83→3.33,
newcomer_coverage 2.33→2.67, mean 3.57→3.81, lint warnings 47→0. Flat:
landing 3.17→3.17 and listenability 2.67→2.83 — the two dimensions the A/B
weighs most. Notably, landing-missing-symbol warnings fell 14→0 while judged
landing did not move: agents now name a symbol in `<code>` to satisfy the
checker without landing better. Next iteration changes ONE thing: landing
examples in references/examples.md (see Follow-ups in plans/README.md).
